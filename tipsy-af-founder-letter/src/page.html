Hey,

In 2013 I helped launch what would become the world's largest mushroom research and ingredient company. It went on to raise over $220 million to bring functional mushrooms to the mainstream.

Like anyone building a career, I said yes to everything. Every trip. Every client dinner. Being on the road took its toll, and I kept telling myself I'd get healthy once things slowed down. They never did.

I was sick of the hangovers. But I didn't want to give up the part I actually liked.

The part I liked was the first drink: shoulders drop, the conversation gets easier, the night opens up. What I didn't like was everything after. The second-guessing, the bad sleep, the next morning.

I'd tried the alternatives. Mocktails taste like a cocktail and feel like a soda. The drinks that do give you a feeling usually get it from kava, kratom or THC, and that's not something I wanted to hand my friends.

So I put my mushroom background to the test

After one too many hangovers, I set out to see if I could create a better buzz. I spent the next five years on what felt like an impossible mission: hundreds of test formulas, brain wave studies, versions we gave to heavy drinkers and lightweights, and plenty of runs we killed because they didn't work.

Then one day I randomly called my dad and told him, “I think I just reverse engineered alcohol.”

After some laughs and some confusion, he tried it. Then he came out of retirement as CEO of that same mushroom company and co-founded TIPSY AF with me.
