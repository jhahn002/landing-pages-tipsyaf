# Assets needed for the v2 design

The preview runs on stand-ins from gettipsy.com. Items are ordered by impact. Sizes are minimums.

## Needed before launch

| # | Asset | Spec | Where it's used | Stand-in today |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Can cutouts, both flavors** | Transparent PNG or WebP, front-facing, same angle, lighting and scale, can at least 2000px tall, soft contact shadow as a separate layer if possible | Product cards, Mix card, buy box | White-background shots blended into the grey (the Pineapple one shows a faint box and slightly different scale) |
| 2 | **Mixed pack shots** | Transparent or plain background: 6-pack (3 + 3) and 12-pack (6 + 6), 2000px wide | Mix card and buy box once the mixed products exist; also the Shopify product images | Two single cans side by side |
| 3 | **Hero image** (or a 6–10s silent loop) | Real photo of the drink in use: people at dinner, on a rooftop, at a party, can visible. Landscape 2400×1350 with the subject in the right 60% for desktop, plus a 1200×900 crop with the can centered for phones. Video as MP4 under 4MB with a poster frame. Match your Meta ad creative so the page continues the ad | Hero | Product still life at night (`TIPSY_wide.jpg`) |
| 4 | **Three "moment" photos** | Real people, 4:5, 1200×1500: (a) after work at home, (b) night in or dinner with friends, (c) weekend afternoon outdoors. Can or glass clearly visible. Not AI-generated | "For the moments you'd usually pour a drink" | Pouring shot, night still life, low-res martini shot (402px) |
| 5 | **Founders photo, high-res** | Same shot of you and your dad, at least 1600px wide (current file is 336px) | Founder section | `founders.png` at 336px, soft on phones |

## Photo direction (to keep it in the Loop-like system)

- Natural light or clean flash, real skin, real settings. Avoid heavy filters.
- Leave empty space on one side of the hero so text can sit there.
- Keep the can label readable in at least one shot of each set.
- Backgrounds in warm neutrals or deep night blues, so the yellow accent and the orange/yellow flavor caps stand out.
