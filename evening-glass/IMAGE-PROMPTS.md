# Image prompts: "Your 5pm glass" and the other "What brings you here?" pages

Prompts for an image model that accepts a reference image (Nano Banana / Gemini, Higgsfield, GPT image,
etc.). Attach a clean photo of the can as the reference, then paste **Product lock + Style + the
concept prompt**. Each concept says which page slot it fills and the aspect ratio that slot uses.

## Before you start

**Reference images to attach** (store Files, front-facing, plain background work best):
- Blood Orange Margarita: `Blood_Orange_Margarita_Orange_BG.png` or `Blood_Orange.png`
- Pineapple Martini: `Pineapple_Martini_Yellow_Orange_Bg.png`
- Optional style reference (mood and light only, not the product): `weavy-Gemini_2.5_Flash_Nano_Banana_-2025-10-13_at_13.42.14.jpg` (the warm margarita pour on the page now)

**Product lock** (paste first, every time):

> Use the attached can as the exact product. Keep its shape, proportions, white matte finish, black
> TIPSY AF wordmark and the colored band at the top exactly as in the reference. Do not redesign,
> rewrite, mirror or add text to the label. One can unless the prompt says otherwise, label facing the
> camera and readable.

**Style** (paste second):

> Photorealistic lifestyle product photography, shot on a full-frame camera, 50mm lens, shallow depth
> of field, natural light. Warm 5pm golden-hour light with soft amber highlights and deep teal or
> charcoal shadows. Real condensation on the can, clear ice, a calm grown-up home setting. Clean,
> uncluttered composition. No text, logos or watermarks besides the can label.

**Liquid color:** Blood Orange Margarita pours a translucent red-orange; Pineapple Martini pours a
translucent golden yellow. Add the right line to any pour shot.

**Leave out of every image:** wine bottles, beer, liquor bottles or anything that reads as alcohol
other than the glassware; car keys or cars; anyone who could look under 25; text overlays; extra or
melted cans; a warped or misspelled label.

## Concepts for the 5pm page

### 1. The 5pm pour: hero, phone (3:4, about 1200×1600)
> A can pouring a steady stream of red-orange drink into a stemmed wine glass filled with clear ice,
> on a warm wooden kitchen counter. Low late-afternoon sun comes through a window behind, backlighting
> the stream and the glass. Blurred home kitchen in the background. The can is in the upper third, the
> glass in the lower middle, with soft empty space at the bottom of the frame for a headline.
> Vertical 3:4.

### 2. Golden-hour counter: hero, desktop (16:9, about 2400×1350)
> Wide shot of a kitchen island at golden hour. On the right third, the can stands beside a stemmed
> wine glass of red-orange drink over ice, a few blood orange slices on a small board. The left half
> of the frame is calm, softly lit, out-of-focus kitchen with warm light and gentle shadows, kept
> empty for a headline. Horizontal 16:9.

### 3. Couch hour: steps section (4:3, about 1600×1200)
> An adult's hand setting a stemmed wine glass of red-orange drink over ice on a low coffee table, the
> can beside it. A linen couch, a folded throw and a warm table lamp just switched on behind, dusk
> blue light in the window. Only the hand and forearm are in frame. Relaxed, end-of-the-workday mood.
> Horizontal 4:3.

### 4. Shoes off: alternate steps / campaign card (1:1, about 1500×1500)
> Close-up of a side table by the front door at dusk: the can and a stemmed glass of red-orange drink
> over ice, a closed laptop and a pair of sneakers kicked off on the floor below, warm lamp light.
> No people. Square 1:1.

### 5. Balcony at sunset: reviews collage, square photo (1:1)
> Two stemmed wine glasses of red-orange drink over ice on a city balcony ledge at sunset, the can
> between them in sharp focus, a folded blanket on a chair behind, warm sky and soft city bokeh.
> No people (this photo sits beside customer quotes). Square 1:1.

### 6. Dinner prep: reviews collage, wide photo (about 1.9:1, 2200×1150)
> A kitchen counter during dinner prep at dusk: the can and a stemmed glass of red-orange drink over
> ice in sharp focus at the front left, a cutting board with sliced citrus and herbs, a pan softly
> steaming out of focus behind. Warm pendant light. No faces. Wide horizontal.

### 7. Pineapple version of the pour: lineup or a second hero (3:4)
> Use the Pineapple Martini can as the reference. The can pouring a golden-yellow drink into a chilled
> coupe glass with a thin pineapple wedge on the rim, on a dark stone counter, warm side light at
> sunset, deep charcoal background. Vertical 3:4.

## After generating

1. Generate 4 or more per concept and keep the ones where the label is crisp, straight and spelled
   right. Zoom in on the wordmark before choosing.
2. If the label drifts, run an edit pass: "Replace the can with the attached reference can exactly,
   keep everything else."
3. Check the liquid color matches the flavor, the ice looks real, and there are no extra fingers or
   floating objects.
4. Upscale heroes to at least 2400px on the long side. Upload to **Shopify → Content → Files** and
   pick them in the theme editor (TIPSY hero, TIPSY steps, TIPSY reviews collage, TIPSY use cases).
5. Write alt text that describes the scene, for example "A can of TIPSY AF Blood Orange Margarita
   poured over ice into a wine glass at sunset".
6. Use generated people only as lifestyle scenes. Don't place them next to a customer quote or present
   them as customers; the quotes on the page are real customers and should stay that way.
