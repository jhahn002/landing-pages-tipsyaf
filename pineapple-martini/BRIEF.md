# Pineapple Martini — Meta landing page brief

Status: angle proposed, awaiting approval. Nothing built or published.
Research date: 2026-10-13.

## Working brief

- **Product and primary action:** TIPSY AF Pineapple Martini. Buy one product; default offer = 6-pack Starter Kit trial ($29.94), which renews as a 12-pack auto-ship. The 12-pack auto-ship ($49.80) and one-time ($60.00) options are secondary.
- **Visitor's situation:** Arrives cold from Meta. Likes the ritual and feeling of a drink but wants to drink less or skip the next-day cost. Has likely tried mocktails that "feel like nothing" and may know of kava/kratom drinks and be wary of them.
- **Incoming promise (assumption — no ad supplied):** "Feels like you're a cocktail or two in, without the booze."
- **Recommended angle:** The first-drink feeling with nothing grey in the can. Real effect (versus mocktails) and clean, legal ingredients (versus kava/kratom/THC), with the 100-day guarantee taking the "what if I feel nothing" risk.
- **Main objection and response:** "Buzz drinks don't work for me." → Effect timeline in plain words, stacking guidance, customer quotes beside the claim, 100-day money-back guarantee (refund even if opened).
- **Offer and material terms:** See "Open conflicts" — renewal price, shipping, reminder day and cancellation window must be confirmed before launch.
- **Brand cues:** LEMON MILK bold display caps (theme asset), Sofia Pro body (Shopify CDN), Coolvetica Hv Comp condensed; black/white with yellow #ffe748, orange #ff9448, pill #fcbc4c, red #d43722; rounded cards; full-bleed yellow pineapple hero with tilted can.
- **Format:** Focused product page, mobile-first (traffic is Meta; the current /pages/pineapple-martini is mobile-only).

## Open conflicts / launch blockers

1. **Trial renewal price:** PM page and Shopify plan = $47.88/mo + free shipping; homepage = $49.80/mo + $2.99 shipping.
2. **Trial reminder day:** PM page Day 24, homepage Day 25.
3. **Cancellation:** "cancel anytime" vs. policy "cancel anytime after the first 7 days".
4. **Servings per can:** FAQ says three servings per can; nutrition label says 1 serving per container.
5. **Age:** homepage FAQ says 18+. Confirm the intended age gate/copy.
6. **Claims needing substantiation before use:** "FDA reviewed ingredients", "brain wave studies", "$220 million raised", "patent pending". Left out unless confirmed.
7. **Delivery estimate:** homepage shows "Aug 12th–15th" (stale). No delivery promise will be used until confirmed.
8. **Hosting:** Shopify page template vs. standalone page (affects how the cart add works).
