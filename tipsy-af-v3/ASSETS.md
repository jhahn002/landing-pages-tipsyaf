# Assets for v3

This design leans on imagery more than v2. The preview uses what's on gettipsy.com. Replace in this order:

| # | Asset | Spec | Used in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Hero photo or video** | Phone: portrait 1080×1600, can or drink in the upper half, calm area at the bottom for the headline and pill. Desktop: landscape 2400×1350, subject in the right 55%. Video: 6–10s silent loop, MP4 under 4MB plus a poster frame. Ideally the same shoot as your Meta ads | Hero |
| 2 | **Can renders, transparent** | Both flavors, front-facing, same lighting, at least 2000px tall, PNG/WebP. Mine are cut from 800px shots and the Blood Orange is soft. The Blood Orange render also reads "MARGARTIA"; check the artwork | Pill cards, lineup, quiz, buy box, ingredient visual |
| 3 | **Three campaign images** | 4:5, 1200×1500, strong single subject with space at the top for big type: (a) a pour or first sip, (b) a night-out moment, (c) both cans or a mixed pack | Campaign cards |
| 4 | **Five use-case thumbnails** | 3:2, 600×400, real people: after work at home, night out, swapping a drink at home, a first try, hosting friends | "What brings you here?" rows |
| 5 | **Mixed pack shot** | 3 + 3 and 6 + 6, transparent background | Mix card, lineup, buy box |
| 6 | **Customer photos or videos** | 4–6, with permission; vertical video 9:16 or square photos | "The buzz is real" collage |
| 7 | **Founders photo, high-res** | At least 1600px wide | Founder block |
| 8 | **Exploded / ingredient render (optional)** | A 3D or styled shot of the can with mushrooms and botanicals floating around it, transparent | Replaces the can-plus-pills visual in the science section |
| 9 | **Review data (optional)** | Export from your review app; needed before showing stars or a review count like Loop's "14 million fans" | Proof headline |
