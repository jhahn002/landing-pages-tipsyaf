Pack builder concepts

Internal mockups for picking two six-packs. Flavors 3–5 are placeholders. Not connected to the cart.

1. Fill the case

A top-down 12-can case. Each "Add 6" drops a six-pack's can tops into the next empty half, so people literally see the box fill up. Tap a half to empty it.

2. Two slots

Two empty six-pack slots with a plus. Tap a slot, then pick a flavor from the strip. The obvious "fill these two boxes" pattern, and it works with any number of flavors.

3. Swipe + tray

Keeps today's dark swipe shelf. Swipe to a flavor, tap "Add six-pack", and it drops into a two-part tray underneath. Feels like building a cart in an app.

4. Counter list

A plain list with − / + steppers counting six-packs, and a ring that fills in each flavor's color. The most scalable: works the same with 3 flavors or 12.
