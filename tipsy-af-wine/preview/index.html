Questions

Does it taste like wine?

No, and it isn't trying to. It's a sparkling zero-proof cocktail. Blood Orange Margarita is bright citrus with a salted edge; Pineapple Martini is pineapple with a dry martini finish. What it replaces is the moment and the feeling, not the flavor.

Why not just alcohol-removed wine?

Alcohol-removed wine keeps the taste of wine and drops the feeling. TIPSY AF is built the other way around: it's made to give you that first-glass ease, without alcohol.

How many should I have in an evening?

One can is one serving and lasts about an hour. Start with one, give it 10 to 15 minutes, and have another if you want to keep it going.

Can I drive after drinking it?

We don't recommend it. Your blood alcohol will be zero, but you can still be considered under the influence. Once the feeling has passed, you can drive.

Who is it for?

TIPSY AF is designed for adults 18 and over. Everything in it is legal, and we ship to all 50 states.
